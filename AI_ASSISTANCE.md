# AI assistance and human responsibility

CMIS Formal Verification is an **AI-assisted research and engineering project**.

Paul Ceronio defines the programme objectives, approves the public/private boundary, decides which mathematical families are reviewed, authorises publication, and remains responsible for the claims made in this repository.

AI tools, including OpenAI systems, have assisted with activities such as:

- research planning and source triage;
- drafting and reviewing documentation;
- workflow and script generation;
- test design and failure analysis;
- theorem-to-family scope comparison;
- register maintenance; and
- preparation of public release materials.

AI-generated text, code or analysis is **not treated as mathematical proof or independent evidence**. A formal theorem receives a PASS only when the pinned workflow reaches the required Comparator, observed-axiom and Lean-kernel gates and preserves a complete receipt. Family-level validation additionally requires that the verified target set covers the complete recorded mathematical scope.

The underlying mathematical sources remain attributed to their original public source repository and authors. CMIS does not claim authorship or novelty of externally sourced mathematics merely because AI-assisted work reproduced, inspected or verified it.

## Disclosure rule for future work

Material added to this repository should disclose substantial AI assistance when AI materially contributed to code, documentation, analysis, review or publication. Contributors remain responsible for checking the resulting work, citing sources, identifying limitations and correcting errors.

## Public/private boundary

AI assistance does not relax the confidentiality boundary. Private CMIS, CBI, TBC, Gmail, customer, investor, credential, commercial or personal material must not be submitted to this public repository.
